Disk measurements on the Linux VM (Google Compute Engine n2-standard-16, us-central1-a).
Taken with fio 3.36 on the boot disk, after the capacity matrix. Purpose: how fast can this disk flush small
writes, since every event needs several synchronous database commits.

Commands (4 KiB random writes, O_DIRECT):
  1 writer, flush after every write:    fio --rw=randwrite --bs=4k --fsync=1 --direct=1 --numjobs=1  --runtime=10
  16 writers, flush after every write:  fio --rw=randwrite --bs=4k --fsync=1 --direct=1 --numjobs=16 --runtime=15
  raw ceiling, deep queue, no flush:    fio --rw=randwrite --bs=4k --direct=1 --iodepth=64 --ioengine=libaio --numjobs=4 --runtime=15

                                          boot disk 50 GB (SSD)    boot disk 500 GB (SSD)
  1 writer, flushed (writes/s)                    850                      930
  16 writers, flushed (writes/s)                6,715                    9,131
  raw 4 KiB random-write ceiling (writes/s)     7,615                   23,400

The disk was resized live from 50 GB to 500 GB in the console and the filesystem grown, with nothing else changed.
Other checks on the 50 GB disk: a plain synchronous dd of 1,000 x 4 KiB took 1.18 s (about 1.2 ms per write);
CPU steal since boot: 0.000%; the platform database was 39 MB with no dead rows after the runs; no live-migration
maintenance event was visible when checked.
